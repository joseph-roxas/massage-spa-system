# Government Deductions stop at a Take-home Floor and the rest is Deferred

The shop remits the full amount every month, so a skipped deduction is money the owner has advanced to the Therapist. In practice the Receptionist skipped deductions in slow weeks and was meant to double them later, which went untracked: the 2026 sheet shows 20 skipped weeks and no doubled ones. Each Therapist Payroll now takes only that week's deductions, down to the Take-home Floor, and keeps the rest as Deferred Deductions, listed by week on every Payslip. The balance comes off the last working week's pay or the Year-end Bonus, or sooner if the Therapist asks; it is never collected automatically, because the next week may be slow too (owner's decision, 2026-11-07, replacing the first version's automatic collection). A week away with no work, for example sick or gone home to the province, still owes its Government Deductions, so all of it is deferred; someone who has left or not yet started owes nothing for that week. We rejected always deducting in full, which the owner will not do to staff in slow weeks, and leaving it to the Receptionist's judgment, which is what failed. Only the Super Admin can override this on a Payroll.
