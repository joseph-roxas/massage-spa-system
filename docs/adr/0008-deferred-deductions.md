# Government Deductions stop at a Take-home Floor and the rest is Deferred

The shop remits the full amount every month, so a skipped deduction is money the owner has advanced to the Therapist. In practice the Receptionist skipped deductions in slow weeks and was meant to double them later, which went untracked: the 2026 sheet shows 20 skipped weeks and no doubled ones. Each Therapist Payroll now takes what is owed down to the Take-home Floor, keeps the rest as Deferred Deductions, collects it from later Payrolls without anyone remembering, and takes any remainder from the Year-end Bonus. We rejected always deducting in full, which the owner will not do to staff in slow weeks, and leaving it to the Receptionist's judgment, which is what failed. Only the Super Admin can override this on a Payroll.
