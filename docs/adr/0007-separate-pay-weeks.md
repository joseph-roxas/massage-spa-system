# Therapists and the Receptionist have different Pay Weeks

Therapist Pay Weeks run Monday to Sunday and the Receptionist's run Sunday to Saturday, because that is how the shop has always paid them. We kept both rather than moving everyone to one week: the owner's 2026 records and staff expectations follow these weeks, and Therapists are paid on Commission, so nothing requires the two to line up. A reader will expect a single Pay Week; this difference is deliberate.
