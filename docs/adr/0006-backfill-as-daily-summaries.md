# Pre-go-live history is kept as Daily Summaries, not as made-up Sales

To complete 2026 the owner only has daily totals, so everything before Go-live is stored as Daily Summaries, a record of its own that reports, the Income Statement, Royalty and Percentage Tax add to real Sales. We rejected generating fake Sales from those totals: they would need invented Guests, Bookings and Therapists per Service, which would corrupt Guest Records, Regulars, Rotation history and Commission, and could never be told apart from real data afterwards.

## Consequences

- Every figure spanning Go-live has two sources; anything without Service or Guest detail (top Services, Discounts by type) is simply not available for those days and says so.
- A day has either a Daily Summary or real Sales, never both, so nothing is counted twice.
